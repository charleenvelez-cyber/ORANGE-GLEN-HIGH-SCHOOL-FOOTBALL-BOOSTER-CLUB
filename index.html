About Our Organization

Supporting student-athletes in Escondido through financial stewardship, volunteer dedication, and educational backing.

Our Mission

To provide financial grants, protective safety equipment, pre-game nutritional meals, and academic assistance to all football players at Orange Glen High School.

Our Vision

To eliminate financial barriers so that every student-athlete in our community can safely participate in football, build lifelong discipline, and earn academic opportunities.

Our Core Values

Student welfare and safety, transparent non-profit governance, equal opportunity regardless of income, and community-wide athletic excellence.

Key Booster Programs

Direct grants and volunteer initiatives engineered to support our high school athletes.

🛡️

Safety Gear & Reconditioning

Funding NOCSAE-certified helmet reconditioning, modern impact-resistant shoulder pads, and protective mouthguards for Varsity, JV, and Freshman teams.

🍎

Nutrition & Game-Day Meals

Providing high-protein pre-game meals, sideline electrolyte hydration stations, and post-game recovery nutrition for away and home matches.

🎓

Academic & Scholarship Grants

Facilitating study hall tutoring, summer conditioning assistance, and awarding annual college scholarship stipends to graduating Patriot senior players.

Our Strategic Approach

How we maximize community donations to drive sustainable athletic and educational impact.

1. Zero Pay-to-Play Friction

We believe no student should sit out due to equipment fees. We provide gear assistance so every student-athlete can put on the Patriot uniform regardless of family income.

2. Safety Equipment Capital Allocation

Player welfare takes priority over everything. Over 50% of our annual operational budget goes directly toward helmet re-certification and protective gear upgrades.

3. Transparent Volunteer Governance

Operated entirely by dedicated parent and community volunteers. 100% of public contributions directly fund team supplies, meals, travel, and student scholarships.
