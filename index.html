Our History & Legacy in Escondido

Orange Glen High School, located at 151 E Pennsylvania Ave, Escondido, CA, has long been a proud cornerstone of North San Diego County athletics. The Patriot Football Program instills values of perseverance, leadership, respect, and academic accountability in high school athletes.

Recognizing that state and district funding only provides for baseline equipment and facility usage, dedicated parents, alumni, and community leaders incorporated the Orange Glen High School Football Booster Club as an independent non-profit entity (EIN: 81-2112629).

Today, our non-profit organization ensures that every student-athlete—regardless of financial background—has access to certified protective gear, varsity pre-game nutrition, digital recruiting highlights, and year-round study halls.

PROGRAMS & COMMUNITY IMPACT

Detailed breakdown of core booster initiatives funded directly by donations and community sponsors.

Safety & Equipment

Riddell SpeedFlex & Protective Gear Upgrades

Player safety is our absolute highest priority. The Booster Club funds yearly safety testing, re-certification, and acquisitions of five-star concussion-rated Riddell SpeedFlex helmets, high-density shoulder pads, and custom mouthguards.

100+ Helmets Certified Annually State-of-the-Art Impact Protection
Scholastic Support

Patriot Football Academic Study Hall

To ensure high athletic performance goes hand-in-hand with academic success, the Booster Club finances tutor stipends, study hall materials, and progress monitoring tools. Players maintain minimum GPA standards to retain game-day eligibility.

Weekly Tutoring Sessions NCAA Eligibility Tracking
Athlete Nutrition

Friday Night Team Feeds & Hydration

Proper fueling before Friday night lights is critical for peak performance and recovery. Booster parents coordinate nutritional pre-game meals for all Varsity, JV, and Freshman athletes prior to every home and away game.

100+ Athletes Fed Every Game Week Electrolyte & Energy Support
College Recruiting

Hudl Recruiting Video & Tech Suite

We equip coaches and players with HD video breakdown software (Hudl) to compile individual highlight reels, track opponent analytics, and distribute game film directly to college scouts and recruiters nationwide.
