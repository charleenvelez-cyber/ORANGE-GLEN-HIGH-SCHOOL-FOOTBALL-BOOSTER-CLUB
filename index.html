About Our Booster Club

Supporting Patriot athletic traditions, character development, and equal opportunities for every student-athlete.

Our Mission

To directly enhance the athletic experience of Orange Glen High School football players by supplying certified protection gear, meals, training equipment, and academic resources.

Our Vision

A thriving, inclusive high school football program in Escondido where financial obstacles never stop a student-athlete from playing, learning teamwork, and excelling.

Core Values

Player safety, academic excellence, transparent financial stewardship, community involvement, and building lifelong sportsmanship.

Booster Club Initiatives

Direct funding and volunteer efforts designed to keep our football players safe, focused, and competitive.

Helmet & Safety Equipment Grants

Providing essential funding to purchase, re-certify, and upgrade football helmets, shoulder pads, and modern protective gear for all roster levels.

Game Day Nutrition & Hydration

Organizing balanced pre-game meals, sideline hydration stations, and healthy post-game recovery snacks for home and away games.

Academic Support & Senior Awards

Offering study sessions, college camp registration assistance, and annual academic scholarship stipends for graduating senior players.

Our Operational Approach

How we ensure maximum efficiency, complete transparency, and full community participation.

1. Volunteer Driven

100% of booster club activities are managed by dedicated parent volunteers, coaches, alumni, and local Escondido leaders.

2. Equitable Roster Support

Funding is distributed fairly across Varsity, Junior Varsity, and Freshman levels so every player benefits equally.

3. Local Business Sponsorships

We partner with local Escondido businesses and supporters to organize fundraising campaigns and community banner sponsorships.

4. Responsible Governance

Maintaining strict compliance with non-profit standards, maintaining open account ledgers, and protecting donor trust.
