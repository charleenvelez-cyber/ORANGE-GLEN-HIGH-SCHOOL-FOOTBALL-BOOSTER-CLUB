Commercial E-Commerce Model

The Orange Glen High School Football Booster Club operates this online fan gear retail store as a primary commercial revenue stream. By designing, inventorying, and selling custom apparel, game-day stadium passes, and fan accessories, the Booster Club generates sustainable financial resources directly powering the student-athlete experience.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Fulfillment Center Address: All retail merchandise items are stocked, customized, and dispatched directly from our official fulfillment address at 151 E PENNSYLVANIA AVE, ESCONDIDO CA 92025.

2. Processing Times: Standard in-stock orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Customized jerseys or pre-order tailgate bundles require 3-5 business days prior to shipment.

3. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates:

  • Standard US Ground Shipping: $5.99 (3-5 business days via USPS/UPS).
  • Express Priority Shipping: $14.99 (1-2 business days).
  • Free Campus Pickup: Free pickup available during home game tailgate events or directly from the Booster Club desk at 151 E Pennsylvania Ave, Escondido CA 92025.

4. International Orders: We currently ship exclusively within the 50 United States and APO/FPO military addresses.

Customer Care

Return & Refund Policy

30-Day Retail Return Guarantee: We want you to wear your Patriot gear with pride. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return unwashed, unworn apparel and merchandise within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Exclusions: Customized player jerseys with custom printed names or numbers are non-refundable unless a manufacturing defect exists.

How to Initiate a Return:

  1. Email our customer service team at query@oghsfbc.bond with your order number.
  2. Package your items securely and mail them to:
    Orange Glen HS Football Booster Club - Attn: Returns
    151 E PENNSYLVANIA AVE, ESCONDIDO CA 92025
  3. Refunds will be credited to your original payment method within 3-5 business days of receipt.
