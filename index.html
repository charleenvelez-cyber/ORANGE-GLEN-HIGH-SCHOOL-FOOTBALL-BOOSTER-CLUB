About Our Booster Club

Fostering team pride, athletic excellence, character development, and equal opportunity for every athlete.

Our Mission

To support the athletic and academic pursuits of Orange Glen High School football players by securing equipment, facility upgrades, and player development resources.

Our Vision

Building a self-sustaining athletic community where every student has access to safe, high-quality sports programs regardless of financial background.

Core Values

Sportsmanship, academic accountability, financial integrity, community partnerships, and unwavering support for player wellness.

Our Key Support Programs

Targeted funding and community efforts designed to elevate the Patriot football experience.

Safety & Equipment Grants

Funding certified football helmets, protective padding, practice gear, and training tools to maximize player safety.

Player Nutrition & Meals

Providing pre-game meals, hydration stations, and healthy recovery snacks to keep athletes performing at their best.

Academic & Camp Sponsorships

Offering merit and need-based camp stipends, tutoring resources, and senior scholarship funds to promote higher education.

Our Operational Approach

Ensuring transparent management, community involvement, and effective stewardship.

1. Volunteer Driven

100% of booster operations are led by dedicated parents, alumni, and community supporters who donate their time.

2. Equitable Allocation

Ensuring resources directly benefit varsity, junior varsity, and freshman squads across all grade levels.

3. Community Partnerships

Teaming up with local businesses in Escondido, CA to raise sponsorships and organize fund-raising drives.

4. Transparent Governance

Maintaining strict compliance, open financial reporting, and compliance with non-profit oversight standards.
