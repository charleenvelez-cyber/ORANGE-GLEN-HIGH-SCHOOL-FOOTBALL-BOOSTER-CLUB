Full Privacy Policy

Organization: ORANGE GLEN HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL BOOSTER CLUB (EIN: 81-2112629) | Domain: oghsfbc.bond

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Orange Glen High School Football Booster Club ("we," "our," or "us") manages and operates the website located at oghsfbc.bond. We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the personal privacy of all student-athletes, parents, volunteers, donors, alumni, and website visitors.

1. Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Policy governs all personal data collected through oghsfbc.bond, official email correspondence sent to query@oghsfbc.bond, digital inquiry forms, donation submissions, and booster club event registrations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information voluntarily provided by users when engaging with our organization, including:

  • Contact Details: Full name, phone number, physical street address, and email address.
  • Student Program Data: Student-athlete name, academic grade level, gear/size specifications, and athletic team division (Varsity, JV, Freshman).
  • Financial & Contribution Records: Transaction receipts, banner sponsorship details, and gear grants (payment processing is handled via secure PCI-compliant third-party providers; full credit card credentials are never stored on our servers).

3. How We Use Collected Data

Data collected by the Booster Club is strictly utilized for core operational and non-profit administration tasks, including:

  • Coordinating player safety equipment grants and gear sizing.
  • Organizing pre-game nutritional meals and travel logistics.
  • Distributing game schedules, volunteer duty sign-ups, and booster meeting notices.
  • Fulfilling legal reporting requirements as a non-profit organization (EIN: 81-2112629).

4. Protection of Student Minors

Protecting student-athlete privacy is a primary operational mandate. No personal contact details, home addresses, or private academic data of high school minors are ever published publicly. Media photographs are published exclusively in accordance with Escondido Union High School District guidelines and parental authorization protocols.

5. Strict Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, trade, lease, or monetize personal information under any circumstances. Personal data is never disclosed to commercial advertising networks or data brokers. Data is accessed exclusively by authorized booster board members for direct non-profit operations.

6. Data Retention & Access Rights

Users may request to inspect, update, or permanently delete their contact records from our mailing lists at any time by sending a written notice to our official administrative address or email.
